## Direction contract

THESIS: Sound is physical material you can see refract and spill, not a dark
stage lit from offscreen. Refuses both the category default this project's
own incumbent chose (near-black cinematic recital hall, string-pluck hero)
and the AI-calibration cliché it could fall into instead (warm cream ground
+ serif display + lamplight). User-pinned world, from two concrete
references (own sites): stackht.pages.dev's glossy liquid-glass blob on a
light, fine-line-art ground, and jjtheartist.pages.dev's grain-paper ground,
huge bold grotesk display type, and ragged paint-stroke color bars.

OWN-WORLD: Light, subtly grain-textured paper ground (`#F6F4EF` family, not
pure white), bold geometric grotesk display face (Syne, extra-bold/black
weights) paired with a cleaner grotesk body face (Plus Jakarta Sans or the
existing Inter) — explicitly not a serif, to avoid the warm-cream-serif
default. One glossy, refractive liquid-glass object (CSS backdrop-filter +
SVG turbulence/displacement, or a Three.js refractive sphere — translate
"the lit string" into "the lit glass drop") as the hero's signature object,
with fine flowing curved line-art (translating the current site's "sound
made visible" string motif into 2D field-lines, in the brass/gold
`#D6A84F` the incumbent already owns) radiating from it. Each instrument
owns one saturated paint-stroke color (ragged brush-edge shape, not a clean
rectangle) used as a material, not a decoration: guitar amber, piano
indigo, vocal crimson, tabla terracotta, violin teal, flute forest — a
direct translation of jjtheartist's skill-bars into the product's own real
content (instruments), never arbitrary.

STORY: A parent or prospective student lands, immediately reads this as a
serious, tactile, working practice — glass and ink, not a dashboard — sees
the two real branches and six real instruments, and taps Enroll or Browse
Courses within the first viewport.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Pill nav badge top-left ("Swar Mangal · Mumbai"), small
circular icon button top-right. Big two-line black grotesk headline,
left-aligned, mid-height. The glass-drop object sits right-of-center with
field-lines flowing from it toward the edges of the frame. Below the
headline: one sentence of real fact (two branches, one-to-one, six
instruments — no stat-block/dd-dt grid, per the project's existing
composition rule), then a solid-black pill CTA ("Enroll now") beside an
outlined pill CTA ("Browse courses"). A small "open for enrollment" status
pill sits below the CTAs, echoing stackht's "open to internships" line.

FORM: User-pinned replacement world (two named reference sites), not a
rolled direction — `new-work.md`'s pinned-brief path, concept-seed skipped
by contract since the direction is already locked by the user, not drawn.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the
finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying
its provenance.
